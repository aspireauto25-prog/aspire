OUR STORY

From Humble Beginnings to Industry Leader

Founded in 2010 by automotive enthusiast Michael Rodriguez, AutoElite started with just five premium vehicles in a small downtown location. Our vision was simple: to provide exceptional car rental experiences that combine luxury, reliability, and outstanding customer service.

Today, we operate in 15 cities across 3 countries with a fleet of over 250 vehicles. Our commitment to innovation led us to be among the first to introduce electric vehicles and develop a seamless digital booking experience.
